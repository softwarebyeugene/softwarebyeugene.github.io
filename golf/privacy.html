Privacy Policy for Golf - CardCraft

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

Golf - CardCraft is published by SoftwareByEugene LLC. We designed the app to be privacy-first and to work without collecting personal data.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit to our servers, or sell your personal data.

The app does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, messages, or gameplay analytics.

Tracking and Advertising

Golf - CardCraft contains no advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data Stored on Your Device

Settings, tutorial progress, game progress, statistics, visual preferences, and an optional player display name may be stored locally on your device so the app can function. This local data is not sent to SoftwareByEugene LLC.

Nearby Multiplayer

If you choose nearby multiplayer, the app uses Apple's local-network technologies to connect devices in the same area. Player display names and the game state needed for the current match are shared directly between participating devices. SoftwareByEugene LLC does not receive, collect, or store this information.

Purchases and Apple Services

Golf - CardCraft is sold as a paid app and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions. The App Store purchase and Apple platform features such as local-network permissions are provided by Apple and are governed by Apple's terms and privacy practices. We do not receive or store your payment-card information.

Learn more at Apple Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect personal data, Golf - CardCraft does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if app functionality or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted here with a revised Last Updated date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to eugene@softwarebyeugene.com.
