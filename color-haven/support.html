Color Haven Support

Color Haven is a private, on-device color-by-number collection for iPhone and iPad.

Before contacting support

  • Use Find Next while painting if a small unfinished region is difficult to locate.
  • Open Settings to adjust number visibility, haptic feedback, and session reminders.
  • If a lifetime unlock is missing, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  • Painting progress is stored on the device. Removing the app also removes local progress.

Contact

Email support@softwarebyeugene.com with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and a description of what happened.
