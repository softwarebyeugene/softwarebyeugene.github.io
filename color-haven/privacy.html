Privacy Policy for Color Haven

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Color Haven is made by SoftwareByEugene LLC. It is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal data.

Information stored on your device

Your painting progress, gallery preferences, comfort settings, survey responses, and anonymous product events are stored only in app-private storage on your device. Color Haven does not upload this information to us.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Color Haven reads verified transaction and entitlement status to unlock purchased features. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Tracking, advertising, and analytics

Color Haven contains no advertising or tracking SDKs, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not sell personal information. Product events are used only on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Accounts, cloud services, and sharing

Color Haven does not require an account and does not upload painting activity or progress to a cloud service. The app does not provide a feature that sends your content to other users.

Children

Color Haven does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The latest version will remain available at this URL.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@softwarebyeugene.com.
