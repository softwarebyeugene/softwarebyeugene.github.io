RoadEQ Support

RoadEQ measures cabin road noise and recommends careful equalizer settings for a compatible Tesla Model Y. It never changes vehicle settings automatically.

Before measuring

  • Mount your iPhone securely and arm RoadEQ while parked.
  • Allow microphone and location access. RoadEQ uses location only to derive speed and measurement stability.
  • Pause music and keep the cabin conditions representative of your normal drive.
  • Drive normally; do not interact with the app while the vehicle is moving.

If RoadEQ keeps waiting or retries

  • Use a steady highway segment with minimal acceleration or braking.
  • Check that the phone remains securely mounted and the microphone is unobstructed.
  • A retry is expected when speed changes or a sample is interrupted.
  • Review and apply any recommended equalizer settings only after stopping.
