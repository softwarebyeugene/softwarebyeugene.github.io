JumpPods

Privacy Policy

JumpPods does not collect any personal data.

Motion processing

JumpPods reads motion samples from compatible headphones to estimate airtime and jump height. Motion samples and results are processed on your iPhone, remain in memory for the current app session, and are not stored or transmitted by JumpPods.

Accounts, analytics, and advertising

JumpPods has no accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking, remote service, or cloud storage. We do not sell or share personal data.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store, device, or platform-level information under Apple’s own terms and privacy practices. JumpPods does not receive your payment-card information.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@softwarebyeugene.com.
