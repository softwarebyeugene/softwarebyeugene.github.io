JumpPods Support

For help, email support@softwarebyeugene.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, headphone model, app version, and a description of what happened.

What you need

  • An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
  • Compatible motion-capable AirPods or Beats headphones.
  • Both earbuds connected and worn securely with Automatic Ear Detection enabled.

If JumpPods cannot find your headphones

Confirm they appear as connected in Bluetooth settings, place both earbuds in your ears, and reopen JumpPods. If iOS asks for Motion & Fitness access, allow it so JumpPods can receive the headphone motion stream.

If “Almost ready” stays on screen

Stand normally for a moment with your head upright. JumpPods becomes ready after it receives a stable, non-freefall motion signal from the connected headphones.

Jump safely

Use a clear indoor area with a dry, level, non-slip surface. Make one controlled standing jump and land with both feet. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or instability.

Results are recreational estimates, not precision measurements, professional training data, or medical guidance.
