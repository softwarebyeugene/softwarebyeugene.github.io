ConditionProof Support

ConditionProof creates guided move-in and move-out condition records on your iPhone.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm ConditionProof has camera permission in Settings when capturing photos.
  • Speech and microphone permissions are needed only for optional dictation.
  • Location evidence is optional and can be turned off.
  • Use Restore Purchases from the export screen to refresh an annual entitlement.
  • Keep the app installed until you have shared any report exports you need; reports are stored locally.

Contact

Email eugene@softwarebyeugene.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and a description of what happened. Do not attach private property photos or reports unless they are necessary and you choose to share them.
