Stop Sign Hunter Support

Stop Sign Hunter turns nearby stop signs into an on-device collection game.

Help

  • Grant camera access so the app can recognize stop signs.
  • Grant location access to label captures and show them on the map.
  • Point the camera at a real stop sign and follow the on-screen capture guidance.
  • Use Stash to revisit captured signs, Map to explore where you found them, and Stats to track regional progress.
