Stop Sign Hunter Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

Stop Sign Hunter is designed as a private, on-device game.

Data collection

Stop Sign Hunter does not collect, track, sell, or share personal data. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Camera and photos

Camera access is used to find and capture stop signs. Camera frames and stop-sign recognition are processed on your device. Captured images stay in the app's local storage and are not transmitted by Stop Sign Hunter.

Location

If you grant location access, Stop Sign Hunter uses your location to label captured signs and show your collection on maps and regional statistics. Coordinates stay in the app's local storage and are not transmitted by Stop Sign Hunter.

Apple services

The app displays maps using Apple MapKit. Apple's handling of requests to Apple services is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@softwarebyeugene.com.
