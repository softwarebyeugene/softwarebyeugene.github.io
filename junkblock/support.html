JunkBlock Support

JunkBlock is a privacy-first iPhone camera that adds automatic modesty protection to photos and videos.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm Camera access is enabled in Settings for photo and video capture.
  • Confirm Microphone access is enabled if you want sound in recorded videos.
  • Keep JunkBlock open while a Precision video is being generated.
  • If an imported video cannot be processed, choose it again and keep JunkBlock open until processing finishes.

Contact

Email support@softwarebyeugene.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and a description of what happened.

Do not attach private photos, videos, or protected source media to a support message.
