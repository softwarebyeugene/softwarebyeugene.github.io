JunkBlock Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

JunkBlock is designed to process your photos and videos privately on your iPhone. JunkBlock does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives your camera media.

Camera and microphone

JunkBlock uses the camera to detect body pose and add modesty protection to photos and videos. If you record video with sound, JunkBlock uses the microphone to include audio. Camera frames, body-pose results, and microphone audio are processed on your device and are not uploaded to JunkBlock or used for advertising.

Captures and protected video sources

JunkBlock stores censored photos and videos in its private app storage so you can view them later. They remain there until you remove them or uninstall the app, unless you choose to share a copy through the iOS share sheet.

Every recorded video also creates a protected source stored only in JunkBlock's private app storage. This source allows you to generate a separate Precision video with frame-by-frame tracking. The protected source:

  • is never shown in JunkBlock's gallery;
  • cannot be shared or exported by JunkBlock;
  • uses iOS complete file protection and is excluded from device backups;
  • is deleted after a Precision video is successfully created or when you remove the related Instant video; and
  • remains app-private after canceled or failed Precision generation so you can retry.

Removing the related Instant video deletes its protected source. Uninstalling JunkBlock deletes data kept in the app's storage. Copies that you previously shared are controlled by the destination you selected and are not deleted by JunkBlock.

Photos library

JunkBlock does not browse or read your Photos library. Sharing or saving a capture is initiated by you through Apple's system share interface and is governed by the destination app or service you choose.

App Store purchase

The paid app purchase is processed by Apple. JunkBlock does not receive or store your payment card information or purchase history, and the app contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Data collection, tracking, and advertising

JunkBlock does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. JunkBlock does not sell personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will be reflected in the policy available from the App Store listing.
