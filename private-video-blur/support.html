Private Video Blur Support

Blur faces and heads in videos, directly on your iPhone.

  1. Tap Choose Video and select a video.
  2. Keep the app open while it imports and processes the video. Large files and videos downloading from iCloud can take longer.
  3. Tap Play to preview the processed video in the app. Use the player controls to pause or scrub.
  4. Tap Save or Share to export your copy.

Automatic detection can miss faces or estimate a head incorrectly. Review the result before sharing. Processing does not change the original video.

If an import fails, try a shorter video and check that your iPhone has enough free storage. For a video stored in iCloud, make sure it can finish downloading in Photos.

For help, email support@softwarebyeugene.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. You do not need to send a private video.
