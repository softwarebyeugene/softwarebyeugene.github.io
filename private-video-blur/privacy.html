Private Video Blur Privacy Policy

Private Video Blur does not collect personal data. Videos and face detection are processed on your iPhone. The app does not upload videos, identify people, use analytics, or display ads.

The app accesses only the video you select. A temporary working copy is stored privately on your device and deleted when processing completes, fails, or is cancelled. Processed videos remain in the app until replaced or deleted. Your original video is unchanged.

If a selected video is stored in iCloud, Apple's Photos service may download it to your device. Saving or sharing the processed video happens only when you choose to do so.
